# Aether User Guide

Aether turns **your** lecture notes into study summaries and multiple-choice quizzes. Questions come from the file you upload — not a generic question bank.

---

## 1. Create an account

1. Open the Aether site.
2. Click **Create an account** (or go to Register).
3. Enter your **name**, **email**, and **password** (at least 6 characters).
4. Click **Register**.

You’ll land on your dashboard after sign-up. Already have an account? Use **Sign in** instead.

---

## 2. Find your way around

The top navigation has:

| Link | What it’s for |
|------|----------------|
| **Dashboard** | Quick overview of your spaces and recent uploads |
| **Spaces** | Course folders (e.g. Computer Architecture) |
| **My uploads** | Every file you’ve uploaded, across all spaces |
| **Profile** | Change your name or password |

---

## 3. Create a course space

Spaces keep materials for one course together.

1. Go to **Spaces**.
2. Click **New space**.
3. Enter a **space name** (required), optional **course code** (e.g. CSM 352), and optional description.
4. Pick an accent colour if you like.
5. Click **Create space**.

You’ll open that space automatically.

---

## 4. Upload lecture notes

Supported types: **PDF**, **Word (.docx)**, and **text** (`.txt` / `.md`).

1. Open a space.
2. Click **Upload**.
3. Choose your file.

Aether reads the text from the file and stores that for studying. Large files may take a moment to prepare.

Tips:

- Prefer clear, text-based PDFs (scanned image-only PDFs may not extract well).
- The file needs enough readable text (very short notes may be rejected).

---

## 5. Generate a quiz

From a **space**:

1. Find your material in **Uploaded materials**.
2. Click **Generate quiz**.
3. Wait while questions are created from that file (this can take a little while).
4. You’ll be taken straight into the quiz.

From **My uploads** or the material’s detail page, you can also generate or open quizzes.

After a quiz exists:

- **Attempt quiz** — take the latest quiz  
- **New quiz** — generate another set from the same file  

---

## 6. Take a quiz

1. Answer each multiple-choice question.
2. Move through the quiz with the navigation controls.
3. Submit when you’re done.
4. Review your score and explanations.

You can retake quizzes later from the same material.

---

## 7. Manage your materials

- Click a material’s **title** to open its detail page (study notes, excerpt, quiz list).
- **Remove** deletes that upload and any quizzes made from it (you’ll be asked to confirm).
- Deleting a **space** removes the space folder; your uploads stay under **My uploads** as unfiled.

---

## 8. Profile settings

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Update your **name** and click **Save name**.
3. To change your password: enter current password, new password, and save.

---

## Quick start checklist

1. Register / sign in  
2. Create a space for your course  
3. Upload a lecture PDF or notes  
4. Click **Generate quiz**  
5. Attempt the quiz and review results  

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| Sign-up asks for email confirmation | Check your inbox, or ask your admin to turn off “Confirm email” in Supabase |
| Upload fails | Use PDF / DOCX / TXT / MD; keep the file under the size limit; ensure it has readable text |
| Generate quiz is slow or fails | Wait a bit and try again; very long documents take longer; ask your admin if AI keys are configured |
| Can’t find a file | Check **My uploads**, or open the space you uploaded it into |
| Forgot password | Use Supabase / site recovery if enabled, or ask your admin for help resetting access |

---

## Privacy note

Your spaces, uploads, and quizzes belong to **your account**. Other students don’t see your materials.

---

Happy studying.
